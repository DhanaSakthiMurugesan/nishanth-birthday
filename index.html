our little universe · April 18 & April 28

To my Kunju… 🖤

Happy birthday to my Kunju, my favourite person, and the one who brought a kind of happiness back into my life that I had missed for so long. ❤️

Can you believe we were schoolmates from LKG to 2nd standard? We weren’t even in the same class, and back then, neither of us could have imagined that after 19 years, life would bring us back together. Then came April 18 — the day we met again — and April 28, the day our story turned into love. Sometimes I think life quietly writes the most beautiful stories while we’re busy living our own. Ours feels like one of them. 🌙

And this birthday is extra special to me, because it’s the birthday of my Kunju. It’s not just another date on the calendar; it’s the day someone who means so much to me came into this world. I’m so happy I get to celebrate you, love you, and be beside you as we walk towards becoming life partners. 🥹

There’s something you should always remember: after five years, you made me smile and laugh like this again — genuinely, without pretending to be okay or being fake. You gave me moments where I could just be myself. And that means more to me than I can ever fully explain. I can’t take credit away from you for that, Kunju. You have brought so much light into my life just by being you. ❤️

That little sunflower memory 🌻 and our first picture together at the beach will always be special to me. They may look like simple moments to anyone else, but to me, they’re pieces of our story — little memories I’ll keep close to my heart.

I don’t expect every day of our future to be perfect. I just hope that, through the easy days and the difficult ones, we keep talking, laughing at silly things, holding each other close, and choosing us. I want to celebrate your wins, support you when things feel heavy, listen to you when you need someone, and build a home filled with comfort, honesty, and love with you.

I hope this new year of your life brings you peace, good health, success, courage for every dream you’re chasing, and a thousand reasons to smile. And when life gets busy, I hope you never forget that there’s a girl who is so grateful that the little boy she once went to school with became her favourite person and the one she wants to share her future with. 🖤

Happy birthday, my Kunju. Thank you for coming back into my life, for the real smiles, for the memories we’ve already made, and for all the ones waiting for us. If I could give you one thing, I’d let you see yourself through my eyes for just a moment — then you’d understand why you’re so precious to me.

From schoolmates, to strangers for years, to finding each other again, to falling in love, and now dreaming of a lifetime together… I’d choose this story with you again. And I’d choose you, too. Always. 🥹❤️
Forever yours, with all my love. ♡
